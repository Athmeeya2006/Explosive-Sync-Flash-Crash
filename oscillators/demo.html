Every oscillator family, live

A population of oscillators of the chosen family, simulated in your browser with the same equations as oscillators/zoo.py (checked by validate_demo.py). Not evidence: the measured results are on index.html and reference.html.

Changes the equations of every unit. The population restarts from a calm state.
How strongly each unit is pulled toward the population average. For theta neurons it is J, the synaptic strength.
Multiplies the coupling by R to the power p. p = 0: plain coupling. p = 1 or 2: units listen more when the others already agree, which makes synchrony explosive and bistable.
Strength of the independent random kicks each unit receives.
Population size. Larger is smoother but slower. Changing it restarts the population.
How many integration steps are taken between two drawings. Higher runs the simulation faster.
Start calm: random phases (R near 0). Start synchronised: every unit at the same phase (R = 1). Shock: puts 30 percent of the units at the current mean phase, like a piece of news that a third of traders act on. Halt: switches coupling off for 20 time units (a circuit breaker), then back on. Sweep: ramps the coupling from 0 to the top of the slider and back, drawing R against coupling in the bottom-right plot (a hysteresis loop when the two directions differ). Pause stops and restarts time. Clear plots erases both history plots.

The population on the circle

Each dot is one unit, placed at its phase (angle) and, for families with an amplitude, at its relative amplitude (distance from the centre; phase-only families sit on the circle). Colour: dot hue = the unit's natural frequency (blue slow, red fast). White arrow: the mean field R e^(i Psi): its length is R, its direction the average phase.

R over time

The order parameter R (vertical, 0 to 1) over the last stretch of time (horizontal). A jump up is synchrony forming; a drop is it collapsing. Grey bands mark halts.

R against coupling

Every frame adds a point (coupling, R). During a Sweep points going up are green and going down red; outside a sweep they are grey. Dashed verticals: this family's measured thresholds (listed under the family menu).
