# 14_lit_review_markets: sources

Downloaded 2026-10-25 for `docs/LITREVIEW_MARKETS.md` (literature review for Paper B, synchrony and
correlation as crash warnings). Only open-access copies (arXiv, PMC/ORA, PLoS, Nature Scientific Reports,
NBER and CREATES working papers, CFTC) were fetched. Paywalled papers (Kritzman et al. 2011, Easley et al.
2011 and 2012, Longin and Solnik 2001, Corsetti et al. 2005, Pollet and Wilson 2010) were skipped.
